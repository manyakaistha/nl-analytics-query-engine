START WITH THE GRAIN

One row is one sales line.

Lines ≠ orders

An order can include multiple products. Count distinct order_id values for orders; counting rows gives sales lines.

Customers can return

Customer IDs recur across orders. There are 6,584 customers with more than one distinct order in this three-month window.

Targets are monthly

The targets table has one row per region and month. Aggregate sales to that same grain before joining the two tables.

SHARED DEFINITIONS

How the numbers are calculated

Revenue per line
quantity × unit_price × (1 − discount)

Discounted product revenue. Shipping is separate. The full dataset totals 23,341,186.3890; no currency symbol is assumed.

Average order value
SUM(revenue) / COUNT(DISTINCT order_id)

Divide total revenue by distinct orders, rather than sales lines.

Profit margin
100 × SUM(profit) / SUM(revenue)

Use the supplied profit values. Guard division by zero.

Target attainment
100 × monthly revenue / target_revenue

Compare each region’s monthly revenue with its matching target. Below 100% means the target was missed.

The sales_with_revenue view adds calculated revenue and order_month (YYYY-MM) to every sales line.
